Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about passes, renewal policies, and local offline privacy.

Do passes renew automatically? expand_more
No. Passes are strictly one-time payments. When your pass period expires, it simply ends without charging you again.
What happens when a pass ends? expand_more
You return to Free mode seamlessly. Your library, bookmarks, imported PDFs, and preferences stay fully intact.
Is my reading sent to your servers? expand_more
Not for on-device reading. Audio synthesis is computed locally on your CPU/GPU. Only optional hosted extras (OCR matrix parsing, cloud voice cloning) communicate with our servers via render credits.
Can I use my licence on more than one computer? expand_more
Yes, all passes support up to 3 devices simultaneously (macOS, Windows, Linux, and ChromeOS).
